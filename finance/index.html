Screening methodology

Consistent calculation. EBITDA proxy = reported operating income + cash-flow depreciation and amortization. FCF = operating cash flow − gross capex. Net leverage = (book debt − cash) / EBITDA proxy. Negative leverage indicates net cash.

Required next step. Inspect one-time items, acquisition effects, fiscal calendars, and accounting policies before using these companies as valuation comparables. A filter match is a research candidate, not an investment recommendation or an available acquisition.
